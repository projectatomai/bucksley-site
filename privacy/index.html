Privacy

Bucksley is a Project ATOM project. Your financial data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. There is no Bucksley server.

The app

Bucksley stores your workspace (accounts, balances, transactions, categories, rules, budgets, notes and tags) on your device. It has no account and no server, and sends none of your financial data to us.

Bucksley is read-only. It reads balances and transactions and cannot make payments, transfers or any other change at your bank.

To check for updates, the Mac app downloads a small feed from bucksley.ai. The request carries the app and macOS version, as any web request does, and none of your financial data.

Bank connections

Bucksley connects to financial institutions only through services you set up yourself. With SimpleFIN Bridge or Plaid, your device fetches balances and transactions directly from that service under your own account and keys; their privacy terms apply to what they hold. Bucksley never sees your bank password. On iPhone, Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings are read through Apple’s FinanceKit only with your permission. CSV files you import are read on your device.

Connection secrets (such as a SimpleFIN access URL or Plaid access token) and your Plaid keys are kept in the Keychain, never in the synced workspace.

iCloud sync

To keep your Mac and iPhone in step, Bucksley syncs your workspace through your own private iCloud database. Only you can read it. Apple’s iCloud terms apply. Keychain items sync only if you use iCloud Keychain.

Ask Bucksley

By default, Ask Bucksley uses Apple’s on-device model, so your questions and the data used to answer them stay on your device. If you add your own Claude or OpenAI key, your question and the relevant transactions are sent to that provider to answer it, under your account and their privacy terms. Your key is kept in the Keychain.

Local agent connections

The MCP server runs on your Mac when an agent starts it, and works on the same local workspace. Agents can only read until you turn on Let agents make changes, and every change an agent makes is logged in Settings → Agents. Keys are never passed to agents.

Your chosen agent may send information it retrieves to its AI provider. Review that provider’s privacy terms before connecting.

Where we host

This site runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare serves every page and manages our traffic, so it handles standard request details such as your IP address and browser to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Our images and downloads are stored in Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare handles that data under its privacy policy.

We’re not a Cloudflare sponsor. We chose them because we think they’re the best value and the best service, and we’ll keep using them for as long as that stays true.

Website and support

The site provides information about Bucksley. Hosting providers may process request information to deliver the site. If you contact us, we use the details you send to respond.

If you click Allow analytics, the site loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits, page views and link clicks. Page addresses are sent without query strings, and advertising features are off. If you decline, Google Analytics does not load. Change your choice any time with Analytics settings in the footer.
