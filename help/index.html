Common questions

What is Bucksley?

Bucksley is a free personal finance app for Mac and iPhone, made by Protomota. It brings your bank accounts, cards and investments into one place to track spending, budgets, recurring bills and net worth. It is read-only and has no server: your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

When can I get it?

Bucksley is in development for Mac and iPhone. Join the Protomota newsletter or Discord to hear when the first release ships.

Is it free?

Yes. Bucksley is free and source available, with no account, no trial and nothing to unlock.

How does it connect to my bank?

Through services you sign up for yourself: SimpleFIN Bridge, or Plaid with your own developer keys. On iPhone it can also read Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings through Apple’s FinanceKit. You can also import a CSV export from another finance app.

Can Bucksley move my money?

No. Bucksley only reads balances and transactions. It cannot make payments or transfers, and it never asks for your bank password.

Where is my data kept?

On your devices, and in your own private iCloud database so your Mac and iPhone stay in sync. Bucksley has no server. Connection secrets and AI keys are kept in the Keychain.

How does Ask Bucksley work?

By default it uses Apple’s on-device model, so your question and your data stay on your device. If you add your own Claude or OpenAI key, questions you ask go to that provider.

Which AI agents can work with Bucksley?

Claude Code, Codex and Claude chat, through a local MCP server with 18 tools. Agents can only read until you turn on Let agents make changes, and every change is logged.
