Every account. One honest picture.

Bucksley brings your bank accounts, cards and investments together on your Mac and iPhone: spending, budgets, recurring bills and net worth in one place. It only reads, it never moves money, and your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. There is no Bucksley server.

        features

        Know where it went. Decide where it goes.

        Bucksley does the everyday work of a paid finance app without the subscription or the account: it pulls in your transactions, files them, and shows you how the month is going at a glance.

        Transactions you can actually tidy

        Search and filter everything. Rename a merchant, change a category, split a purchase, add a note or tags, and mark it reviewed. Fix one and Bucksley offers a rule so the next one files itself.

        RuleMerchant contains “bean there” → Dining12 matched
        SplitBig Box Store $182.40 → Groceries $121.15 · Home $61.252 parts

        Budgets that keep pace with you

        Set a budget per category or group. Leftovers can roll into next month, a pace marker shows whether you’re ahead of the calendar, and Bucksley can suggest budgets from what you really spend.

        Groceries$486 of $600 · on pace81%
        Dining$312 of $300 · $12 over104%

        Net worth, by what it’s made of

        Cash, investments, crypto and debt stacked over time, with every account’s balance and trend in the sidebar.

        Recurring, spotted for you

        Bills and subscriptions are found automatically, with what’s due next and which ones quietly went up.

        Ask Bucksley

        Ask about your money in plain words. Answers come from Apple’s on-device model by default, or from your own Claude or OpenAI key if you add one.

        connect

        Bring your accounts. Keep the keys.

        Bucksley talks to your banks through services you sign up for yourself, so the connection belongs to you. Every connection is read-only, and its secret is kept in the Keychain on your device.

        SimpleFIN Bridge

        Link thousands of US banks and cards through your own SimpleFIN Bridge account. Paste its setup token and Bucksley does the rest.

        Plaid, with your keys

        Already have Plaid developer keys? Add them and connect institutions through Plaid’s own sign-in. Bucksley never sees your bank password.

        Apple Wallet

        On iPhone, Bucksley reads Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings through Apple’s FinanceKit, with your permission.

        CSV import

        Moving from another finance app? Import its transaction export, categories and tags included, and keep your history.

        iphone · coming soon

        Review it in a spare minute.

        The iPhone app turns new transactions into a short review inbox. Swipe through them while you wait for coffee, and everything you mark syncs back to your Mac through your private iCloud.

        • Your iCloud, not ours. Your workspace syncs through your own private iCloud database. Bucksley has no server to send it to.
        • Widgets and Shortcuts. Net worth, budgets and what’s left to spend on your Home Screen, plus App Intents for Shortcuts and Siri.
        • Apple Wallet on board. Apple Card, Apple Cash and Savings come in straight from FinanceKit on your iPhone.
        agents

        Let your agent do the bookkeeping.

        Bucksley has an MCP server built in. Connect Claude Code, Codex or Claude chat and it can answer questions about your money, clear the review queue and write rules. Agents can only look until you allow changes, and every change is logged in the app.

        How agent setup works
        Bucksley › Settings › Agents

        File my coffee runs under Dining and make it stick. Then tell me how dining looks this month.

        • set_categoryAgents can view only. Turn on “Let agents make changes” in Settings → Agents.
        • set_category4 transactions → Dining
        • create_ruleMerchant contains “bean there” → Dining
        • get_spendingDining · November · $312 of $300

        privacy

        Your money stays yours.

        Read-only, always

        Bucksley reads balances and transactions. It can’t move money, pay bills or make transfers, and it never asks for your bank password.

        No Bucksley server

        Your data lives on your devices and in your own iCloud. There’s no account to make and nothing for us to store.

        Agents on your terms

        The MCP server runs on your Mac. Agents can only look until you allow changes, and keys are never handed to them. How agent access works

        Bucksley for Mac.

        Free for Macs running macOS 15 or later. Signed by Protomota, notarized by Apple, and kept up to date automatically. The iPhone app is coming soon.

        Download and install MCP setup
        1. Open the disk image and drag Bucksley into Applications.
        2. Connect your accounts with SimpleFIN Bridge or your own Plaid keys, or import a CSV.
        3. Review and budget. Bucksley files your transactions; you set budgets and check the month at a glance.
