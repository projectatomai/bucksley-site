What you’ll need

  • A Mac running macOS 15 or later. The iPhone app needs iOS 18 or later and the same iCloud account.
  • A way in for your accounts: a SimpleFIN Bridge account, your own Plaid developer keys, Apple Wallet on iPhone, or a CSV export.
  • For Ask Bucksley on-device, a Mac or iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Otherwise, your own Claude or OpenAI key.

Bucksley will be free, with no account and nothing to unlock. The source will be available on GitHub.
